Beyond God: Ascension

A collect-and-battle mythology RPG for iOS and Android

Published by Bublue Studio

About the game

Beyond God: Ascension is a free-to-play mobile role-playing game where you summon legendary gods and heroes from four great mythologies — Greek, Norse, Egyptian, and Chinese — and build a team to battle through a world at war. The divine order has shattered, and it is your task to assemble champions such as Zeus, Odin, Ra, and the Jade Emperor, master their elemental synergies, and rise from mortal to myth.

What you do in the game

  • Summon heroes: collect 40+ gods and champions through a fair, pity-backed summon system.
  • Build a team: command a squad of five and combine factions, elements, and skills for powerful synergies.
  • Battle through content: a hundred-chapter campaign, ranked Arena, the Endless Tower, dungeons, and the 72 God Pillars.
  • Grow your power: level, star up, and ascend heroes, and forge mythic relics to reach godlike strength.
