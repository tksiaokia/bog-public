Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I keep my account safe? Open Settings in-game and bind your account to Apple, Google, or Facebook. This lets you recover progress and play on another device.
  • I lost my progress / changed phones. Log in with the same social account you bound. If you never bound one, email us with your in-game ID.
  • My purchase didn't arrive. Restart the app first — rewards are delivered by our server and may take a moment. If it still hasn't appeared, email us with your transaction ID.
  • How do I request a refund? Purchases are handled by Apple. Manage refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • The game won't connect. The game requires an internet connection. Check your network; if the problem persists, email us.
