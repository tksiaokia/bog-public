2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Game and its features;
  • To save, sync, and restore your account and progress;
  • To process in-app purchases and grant entitlements;
  • To display rewarded advertisements and verify ad completions;
  • To run events, rankings, and leaderboards;
  • If you pre-registered on our website: to send you a one-time launch notification and deliver pre-registration rewards to the game account you later sign in with;
  • To detect, prevent, and investigate cheating, fraud, and abuse;
  • To respond to your support requests;
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Advertising

The Game shows rewarded ads that you choose to watch in exchange for in-game rewards. We use Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC) to serve these ads. Google and its ad partners may use your advertising identifier and device data to serve and measure ads; you can read how Google uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can limit or reset ad personalization at any time in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

Where required by law — for example, for users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland — we present a consent message using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before showing personalized ads, and we honor your choice. On iOS, we also ask permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before accessing your advertising identifier; if you decline, you may still see non-personalized ads.

5. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and its Google Play target-audience and content rating are set accordingly. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and ads are served with these settings applied. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data Retention

We keep your account and progress data for as long as your account exists or as needed to provide the Game. We may retain limited records longer where required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it. Pre-registration emails are kept only until launch and are deleted within a reasonable period afterward, or sooner if you ask us to remove them.

7. Your Rights & Account Deletion

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights — including requesting deletion of your account and associated data — email us at the address in Section 10. We will verify your request and respond within the time required by applicable law.

8. Data Security & International Transfers

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Your data may be processed in countries other than your own, including where our service providers operate; where required, we use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, through an in-game notice.
