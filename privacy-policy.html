4. Advertising

The Game shows rewarded ads that you choose to watch in exchange for in-game rewards. Our ad partner may use your advertising identifier and device data to serve and measure ads. You can limit or reset ad personalization at any time in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking). On iOS, we ask permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before accessing your advertising identifier. Where required by law, we request consent for personalized advertising before ads are shown.

5. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data Retention

We keep your account and progress data for as long as your account exists or as needed to provide the Game. We may retain limited records longer where required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.
